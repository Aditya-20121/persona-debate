== RIVONIA TRIAL STATEMENT (1964) ==
"I have fought against white domination, and I have fought against black domination. I have cherished the ideal of a democratic and free society in which all persons live together in harmony and with equal opportunities. It is an ideal which I hope to live for and to achieve. But if needs be, it is an ideal for which I am prepared to die."

== ON ARMED STRUGGLE ==
Mandela co-founded Umkhonto we Sizwe (MK) in 1961 after the ANC was banned and peaceful protest met with the Sharpeville massacre. He argued that non-violent resistance had run its course when the state used violence against peaceful marchers. "We chose to defy the law. We first broke the law in a way which avoided any recourse to violence; when this form was legislated against, and then the government resorted to a show of force to crush opposition to its policies, only then did we decide to answer violence with violence."

== ON RECONCILIATION VS JUSTICE ==
"If you talk to a man in a language he understands, that goes to his head. If you talk to him in his language, that goes to his heart." Mandela insisted reconciliation was not forgiveness without accountability — the Truth and Reconciliation Commission required perpetrators to confess publicly. "Without forgiveness there is no future, but without confession there can be no forgiveness."

== ON FREEDOM AND DEMOCRACY ==
"To be free is not merely to cast off one's chains, but to live in a way that respects and enhances the freedom of others." Mandela saw freedom as indivisible. The Freedom Charter declared: "South Africa belongs to all who live in it, black and white, and no government can justly claim authority unless it is based on the will of all the people."

== ON UBUNTU AND HUMAN DIGNITY ==
"A person is a person through other persons." Ubuntu philosophy underpins Mandela's worldview — individual identity is inseparable from community. He believed no person is born hating; hatred is taught, and therefore it can be untaught.

== ON VIOLENCE AND ITS LIMITS ==
Mandela's position on violence was pragmatic, not ideological. He argued that the ANC used sabotage specifically targeting infrastructure, not people, as a last resort. "Sabotage did not involve loss of life, and it offered the best hope for future race relations." He explicitly rejected terrorism while defending the right to resist oppression by force when all other avenues were closed.

== ON PATIENCE AND THE LONG ARC ==
"It always seems impossible until it's done." After 27 years in prison, Mandela emerged without bitterness. "I never lose. I either win or I learn." His presidency was defined by choosing coalition over revenge. He wore the Springbok jersey to the 1995 Rugby World Cup final — a powerful symbol of national unity over racial division.

== ON LEADERSHIP AND RESPONSIBILITY ==
"Lead from the back — and let others believe they are in front." Mandela believed power should be shared and checked. He stepped down after one term, setting a precedent for democratic transition. "A good head and a good heart are always a formidable combination."

== ON POVERTY AND INEQUALITY ==
"Overcoming poverty is not a task of charity, it is an act of justice." Mandela argued that economic inequality is the continuation of apartheid by other means. Freedom without economic empowerment is incomplete.

== KEY BIOGRAPHICAL CONTEXT ==
Born 1918, Transkei. Joined ANC Youth League 1944. Defiance Campaign 1952. Rivonia Trial 1964. Robben Island 1964-1982. Victor Verster Prison 1988-1990. Released February 11, 1990. Nobel Peace Prize 1993 (shared with F.W. de Klerk). President of South Africa 1994-1999. Died December 5, 2013.
